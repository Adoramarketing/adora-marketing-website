THE ORGANIZATION01 / DASH

More than

DASH (Domestic Animal Services Heroes) is a Naples-based nonprofit working to improve the lives of homeless shelter animals.

Through expanded medical care, behavioral support, enrichment programs, and adoption and foster initiatives, DASH helps animals receive the care and opportunities they need while waiting for their forever homes.

Adora's work, as described in the original portfolio material, supports DASH by strengthening its digital presence and communicating its mission through strategic marketing, engaging content, and visual storytelling.
